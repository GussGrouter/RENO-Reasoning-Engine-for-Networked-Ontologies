# Performance mantras

- Tag: heuristic

## Definition

**Performance mantras** are an ordered checklist of intervention strategies (from most to least effective) for improving performance by reducing, avoiding, deferring, hiding, parallelizing, or cheapening work.

## Relation

- Role: framework (a prioritization structure for “what kind of intervention should we try first?”).
- Connects directly to cross-book concepts:
  - “don’t do it” and “do it less” align with [[optimize-expected-case]] and eliminating unnecessary work.
  - “do it later” aligns with [[shift-computation-in-time]] (deferral off the critical path).
  - “do it, but don’t do it again” aligns with [[caching]] and [[time-space-tradeoff]].
- Fits [[model-classify-intervene]] by suggesting intervention families after you’ve modeled and classified the bottleneck.
