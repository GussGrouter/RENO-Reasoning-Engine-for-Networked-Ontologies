# Coefficient of variation

- Tag: measurement

## Definition

The **coefficient of variation (CV/CoV)** expresses relative variability as a single metric:

\[
\\mathrm{CV} = \\frac{\\sigma}{\\mu}
\]

where \(\\sigma\) is standard deviation and \(\\mu\) is the mean. Lower CV implies less relative variance.

## Relation

- Role: measurement (a normalized variance signal that is comparable across different means).
- Useful when variance must be compared across different scales or units.
