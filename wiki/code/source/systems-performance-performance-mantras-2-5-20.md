## Extracted ideas (with classification)

- (diagnosis) A tuning methodology can be an ordered list of intervention families, from most to least effective.
- (diagnosis) Mantras: don’t do it; do it but don’t do it again; do it less; do it later; do it when they’re not looking; do it concurrently; do it more cheaply.
- (mechanism) Examples map these to common interventions (eliminate work, caching, reduce polling/refresh frequency, write-back caching, off-peak scheduling, concurrency, faster hardware).
