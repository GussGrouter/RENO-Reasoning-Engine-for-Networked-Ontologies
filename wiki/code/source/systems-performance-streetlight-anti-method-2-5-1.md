## Extracted ideas (with classification)

- (diagnosis) Diagnosing performance by picking familiar/random tools is “hit or miss” and can overlook many issue types.
- (diagnosis) This bias is analogous to the streetlight effect: you look where the “light” (tool familiarity) is best, not where the problem is.
- (diagnosis) Findings can be slow to obtain (many unrelated checks), and may be false positives: “an issue but not the issue.”
