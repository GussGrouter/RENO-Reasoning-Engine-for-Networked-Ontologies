## Extracted ideas (with classification)

- (framework) Modeling can simulate large enterprise systems, but large-scale performance is complex and hard to model accurately.
- (method) Cloud environments can be rented for short durations to experimentally test scaling: characterize + simulate workload, then measure real systems across scales.
- (diagnosis) Experimental scaling can reveal knee points and real-world limiters omitted from theoretical models.
