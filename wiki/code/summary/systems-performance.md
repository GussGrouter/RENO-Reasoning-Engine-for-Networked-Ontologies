# Systems Performance

Systems Performance is used to measure and explain system behavior under real workloads so you can find bottlenecks and choose safe changes.

Use this domain to:
- diagnose why a system is slow or unstable
- decide what to measure next (metrics, profiling, tracing, experiments)
- prioritize fixes by impact and risk

- Measurement boundaries: [[observability-vs-experimentation]], [[instrumentation-overhead-and-perturbation]]
