# Harmonic mean

- Tag: measurement

## Definition

The **harmonic mean** is the count of values divided by the sum of their reciprocals. It is often appropriate for averaging **rates** over a fixed total quantity (e.g., average transfer rate across segments with different rates).

## Relation

- Role: technique (a statistic choice for summarizing rates without biasing toward high-rate intervals).
- Pairs naturally with throughput-style metrics in [[throughput-latency-metrics]].
