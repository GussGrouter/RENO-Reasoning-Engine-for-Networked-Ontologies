# Multivariate metric surface plot

- Tag: measurement

## Definition

A **multivariate metric surface plot** encodes more than two data dimensions by mapping dimensions to axes and surface height (and optionally color), useful when values vary smoothly enough to form an interpretable “terrain” of utilization or other metrics.

## Relation

- Role: measurement (a visualization encoding for high-dimensional operational telemetry).
- Often used to compare many entities across time slices (e.g., many CPUs/servers across seconds), complementing simpler time series views.
- Pairs with [[metric-visualization]] and supports interpreting [[utilization-and-saturation]] patterns across fleets.
