# Geometric mean

- Tag: measurement

## Definition

The **geometric mean** is the \(n\)th root of the product of \(n\) values. It is often appropriate for averaging **multiplicative** factors, such as layered performance improvements that compound.

## Relation

- Role: technique (a statistic choice for summarizing multiplicative effects).
- Useful when improvements apply sequentially to the same work (e.g., pipeline/layered processing), where effects multiply rather than add.
