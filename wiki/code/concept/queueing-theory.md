# Queueing theory

- Tag: abstraction

## Definition

**Queueing theory** models systems with queues to analyze how arrival rate, service time, and service capacity determine queue length, wait time (latency), utilization, and response time distributions.

## Relation

- Role: framework (a mathematical basis for diagnosing and predicting queue-driven latency under load).
- Connects directly to [[utilization-and-saturation]]: saturation implies queued work; queueing models relate utilization to response time growth.
- Supports [[model-classify-intervene]] by predicting how latency changes with load and which interventions (reduce arrival rate, reduce service time, add service capacity) will matter.
