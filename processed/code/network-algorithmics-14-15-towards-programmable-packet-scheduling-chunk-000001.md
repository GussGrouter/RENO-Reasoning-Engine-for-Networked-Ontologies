14.15 Towards programmable packet scheduling
We introduced the concept of programmable switches and routers in Section 11.15 in the context of
programmable IP lookups and P4. As mentioned in Section 11.15, P4 currently does not currently
support programmable queueing disciplines (i.e., packet scheduling policies), but considerable research
effort has been expended towards this goal. In this section, we introduce the concept of programmable
packet scheduling and describe two representative research proposals.
